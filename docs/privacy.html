Privacy Policy

Next Time · Last updated October 27, 2026

Next Time is built so your notes and places stay yours. We don't run servers, we don't have accounts, and we don't collect any data about you.

Where your places are kept

The places you save, your ratings, notes, dishes, photos and reminder settings are stored on your iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive turned on, the app also keeps a copy in your own private iCloud storage, so your places come back on a new iPhone and stay in sync across your devices. That copy is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we can't see it. To stop backing up, turn off iCloud Drive for Next Time in Settings.

Sharing a place with a friend

When you choose "Share with a Friend", the app creates a link that contains that place's name, address, location, rating, note and dishes (never your photos). The details are inside the part of the link after the "#", which browsers don't send to any server. Only people you send the link to can see them.

Location

Next Time asks for location access "While Using the App" to show places near you and to remind you when you arrive at a place you saved. Arrival reminders are handled by iOS itself on your device. Your location is never sent to us or stored by the app.

Apple Maps and Google Maps

When you search for a place, list places near you, or look up an address, the request goes to Apple Maps, under Apple's privacy policy. When you paste a short Google Maps link, the app opens that link to find the full place address, which contacts Google under Google's privacy policy. Tapping "Open in Google Maps" opens the Google Maps app or website.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent through a push notification server.

Analytics and tracking

None. Next Time contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code.

Children

Next Time does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions? See the support page.
