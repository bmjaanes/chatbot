Next Time Support

Notes to yourself for the next time you're somewhere.

I'm not getting reminders

  • Open Settings → Next Time and make sure Location is set to While Using the App (or Always) and Notifications are allowed.
  • Check that the reminder's switch is on in the app.
  • If you saved the reminder while standing at the place, iOS usually waits until you leave and come back before it reminds you.
  • Reminders work best with a radius of 150 m or more. Very small areas are less reliable.

How do I add a place from Google Maps?

In Google Maps, open the place, tap Share, then Copy. In Next Time, tap +, choose Google Maps, and tap Paste.

Is there a limit?

iOS lets an app watch up to 64 places at a time. You can save more; turn some off to make room.
